없습니다. 이름, 이메일, 전화번호, 위치, 연락처, 사진, 기기 식별자 등 어떤 개인정보도 수집하지 않습니다.

게임을 이어서 하기 위해 다음 정보를 이용자 기기 안에만 저장합니다. 이 정보는 회사나 다른 곳으로 보내지지 않습니다.

  • 게임 진행 기록(이어하기·저장 칸, 본 결말, 장 기록, 읽은 대사)

  • 정식판을 샀는지 여부

앱을 삭제하면 이 정보도 함께 지워집니다.

3. 제3자 제공 및 처리 위탁

없습니다. 광고나 이용 분석 도구를 넣지 않았습니다.

4. 인터넷 사용

게임은 인터넷 연결 없이 동작합니다. 정식판을 사거나 복원할 때만 앱스토어·구글 플레이·토스와 통신하며, 그 밖의 외부 서버와는 정보를 주고받지 않습니다.

Tell Me Your Name Privacy PolicyEffective November 1, 2026 · Seedhouse

Tell Me Your Name does not collect any personal information. There is no sign-up or login, and your game data stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information.

2. Data stored only on your device

To let you continue playing, the app stores your game progress (save slots, endings seen, chapter records, lines read), your settings (music and sound volume, text speed, skip mode) and whether you own the Full Version on your device. This data is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Third parties

We do not share data with third parties. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. The Full Version purchase is handled by the Apple App Store, Google Play or Toss (Apps in Toss); we never receive your name, card number or other payment details. The app only asks the platform whether the Full Version has been purchased.

4. Internet use

The game works offline. It contacts the App Store, Google Play or Toss only to buy or restore the Full Version, and does not communicate with any other server.

5. Bug report emails

Tapping Bug Report in the game pre-fills an email in your own mail app. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send yourself. The email contains what you wrote plus information needed to fix the problem (app version, device model, OS version and screen size, the in-game scene and recent lines, and a progress code that reopens that scene). We use these emails only to fix bugs and delete them once handled. The sender address is your own email account.

6. Children

Because we collect no personal information, we do not collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page at least 7 days before it takes effect.
